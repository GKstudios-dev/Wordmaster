Welcome to Wordmaster!

Wordmaster is a fun word puzzle game that challenges your vocabulary and spelling skills.

HOW TO PLAY

•Each puzzle displays the first and last letters of a hidden word.

•You have three attempts to guess the correct word.

•The Hint button is initially locked.

•After two incorrect attempts, the Hint button becomes available and reveals one letter from the middle of the word.

•A correct answer without using a hint earns 2 points.

•A correct answer after using a hint earns 1 point.

•Each level contains 10 words.

•You must answer all 10 words correctly to unlock the next level.

•If you answer any word incorrectly, you will replay the current level.

•Completing a level also earns a bonus of 5 points.

Thank you for playing Wordmaster!
